# Rules

EuroLeague Fantasy Challenge — **Classic Mode** (all versus all, non-exclusive rosters).

Sources: the original README rules, extended and corrected against the [official rules GitBook](https://fantaking.gitbook.io/euroleague-fantasy-challenge-rules) (snapshot 2026-09-20). Where the two disagree the GitBook wins — see [Discrepancies](#discrepancies). Other game modes are only summarised, see [Other modes](#other-modes).

## Overview

- Up to **3 fantasy teams** per account, each with **100 credits** to build a roster of 10 players + 1 head coach.
- Rosters are non-exclusive: any player within budget can be picked, even if other users have picked him.
- Every team is automatically entered in the General league; you can also create private leagues.
- Players score from real EuroLeague statistics (regular time plus overtimes) and their team's result.

## Team composition

| Slot       | Count |
| ---------- | ----- |
| Centers    | 2     |
| Forwards   | 4     |
| Guards     | 4     |
| Head Coach | 1     |

- Max **6 players from the same EuroLeague team** (also 6 in the Playoffs phase).
- Every player has a credit value that changes over the season (see [Prices](#prices)).
- The starting five is arranged in one of these formations (guards-forwards-centers): **2-2-1, 1-2-2, 2-1-2, 1-3-1, 3-1-1**.

### Joining after the season has started

- Entry score = average points of all teams, assigned at the end of the Round.
- Budget = 100cr + 0.3cr per Round already played (e.g. registering on the 7th Matchday: 100 + 0.3 × 6 = 101.8cr).
- Creating a team while a Round is in progress: the chosen players do not count for that Round, which gets the average entry score.

## Schedule

- A **Round** (R) is split into **Turns** (T1, T2, T3…): the block of games played on a single day (e.g. T1 = all Tuesday games, T2 = all Wednesday games).
- Regular Season, Play-In, Playoffs and Final Four form **one unified competition**: one set of overall rankings and leagues for the whole season.

## Lineup management

- Between two Turns you can make field–bench substitutions, change the Captain and change the formation — a swap is only allowed if the incoming player has **not yet taken the field**.
- A player moved from the field to the bench gets 50% of his score.
- Before each Turn you can reset all the substitutions you made.
- Tip: line up players who play in T1 at the start of the Round, and spread the rest of the roster over later Turns so a poor T1 can still be swapped out.

## Scoring

### Share of points counted

| Role                               | Counted  |
| ---------------------------------- | -------- |
| Starting five                      | 100%     |
| Sixth man                          | 100%     |
| Head coach                         | 100%     |
| Bench players                      | 50%      |
| Captain (one of the starting five) | score ×2 |

### Player score

Sum of the following actions:

| Action                            | Bonus                                         | Penalty |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Point scored                      | +1                                            |         |
| Rebound                           | +1                                            |         |
| Assist                            | +1                                            |         |
| Steal / Turnover                  | +1                                            | −1      |
| Blocked shot performed / suffered | +1                                            | −1      |
| Foul drawn / committed            | +1                                            | −1      |
| Missed shot                       |                                               | −1      |
| Missed free throw                 |                                               | −1      |
| Team win bonus                    | +10% of the player's fantasy score that Round |         |

The GitBook lists the team win bonus in the _Penalty_ column; it is read here as a bonus.

### Coach score

| Result                              | Points |
| ----------------------------------- | ------ |
| Win by 1–10 points, or in overtime  | +10    |
| Win by 11–20 points                 | +20    |
| Win by 20+ points                   | +25    |
| Loss by 1–10 points, or in overtime | −5     |
| Loss by 11–20 points                | −10    |
| Loss by 20+ points                  | −20    |

The coach scores even if dismissed, absent or disqualified.

## Trades

- The trade market is open between the end of each Round and the start of the next.
- Up to **4 trades per Round**: sell up to 4 players and buy replacements.
- The coach can be traded but counts towards the 4 (so 3 players + coach, or 4 players).
- Trades are **unlimited** in the Play-in, Playoffs and Final Four phases, and in these Regular Season windows:

| After | Window          |
| ----- | --------------- |
| R6    | Oct 16 – Oct 21 |
| R13   | Nov 21 – Nov 24 |
| R18   | Dec 19 – Dec 22 |
| R23   | Jan 16 – Jan 21 |
| R28   | Feb 13 – Mar 4  |
| R34   | Mar 27 – Mar 31 |

## Prices

- After each Round a player's credit value rises or falls (capital gain / loss), driven by two factors: the **score obtained** and the **starting value** — for the same score, a low-priced player gains more than a high-priced one.
- The change adjusts your team's credit budget, i.e. your purchasing power. Example: a 20.5cr player gains +1.5cr and reaches 22cr; the team value goes 100 → 101.5cr, so selling him buys a 22cr replacement.
- Ways to capture gains (GitBook tips): buy injured stars back cheaply on their return, pick mid-priced replacements of injured stars, and find underrated players before their price rises.

## Suspended or changed games

- Moved to a time when the previous Round has ended and the next has not started: valid as normal.
- Moved to a time when the previous Round has not ended or the next has already started: at the end of T1 the players of the teams involved (coaches, injured and disqualified players included) get their **Average Score** (average score of the season so far).
  - Postponed match with average score awarded: the captain does **not** double.
  - Cancelled game during an open Round: the captain keeps the doubled score.
- Special events (many games or a whole Round postponed): the organiser decides and communicates through apps, news and social channels.
