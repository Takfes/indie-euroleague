# Resources

## Official

- [EuroLeague Fantasy Challenge — in-app rules](https://euroleaguefantasy.euroleaguebasketball.net/10/rules) — rules page of the game itself (from the original README; single-page app, not machine-readable).
- [Official rules GitBook](https://fantaking.gitbook.io/euroleague-fantasy-challenge-rules) — full rules, in several languages. Machine-readable index at [llms.txt](https://fantaking.gitbook.io/euroleague-fantasy-challenge-rules/llms.txt); any page is available as Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL.
- [Dynamic new player price changes](https://www.euroleaguebasketball.net/euroleague/news/euroleague-fantasy-challenge-dynamic-new-player-price-changes/) — EuroLeague article on how player prices move.
- [Rules, deadlines, tips](https://www.euroleaguebasketball.net/en/euroleague/news/euroleague-fantasy-challenge-rules-deadlines-tips/) — EuroLeague article on rules, deadlines and tips.
